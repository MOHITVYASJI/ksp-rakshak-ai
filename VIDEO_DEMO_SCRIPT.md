## 💡 Pro Tips for Recording Your Hackathon Video:
1. **Screen Recording:** Use OBS Studio or Camtasia at 1080p 60fps.
2. **Audio:** Use a clean USB microphone for crisp voiceover narration.
3. **Browser Zoom:** Set browser zoom to `90%` or `100%` so all tactical widgets, link graphs, and maps fit cleanly on screen.
4. **Resolution:** Record in 16:9 aspect ratio (`1920x1080`).
